Frequently asked questions

What does BizStack Perks actually do?

We help you find and close local business leads. Our system spots businesses showing public signs of growth or financing needs, our AI phone assistant talks to prospects and can close a signup live on the call, and everything gets tracked in your dashboard and their customer portal.

How does the AI phone assistant work?

It answers your business line, understands speech or touch-tone input, can explain pricing and features, and if a caller wants to sign up, it collects their business name and texts them a secure Stripe checkout link before the call even ends.

How is payment confirmed?

Checkout redirects the buyer back to the site, but fulfillment is confirmed only after the Stripe webhook is verified server-side.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Payments are processed securely through Stripe and we never store your card details. Your contact information is only used to follow up on your request.
