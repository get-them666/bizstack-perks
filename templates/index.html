Frequently asked questions

What does BizStack Perks actually do?

We find and close local business leads for you using AI. Our system discovers businesses showing public signals of growth or financing needs, our AI phone assistant calls them, explains your offer, and can close a sale live on the call. Every interaction—calls, texts, signups—gets tracked in a real-time dashboard so you see exactly what's working.

How many leads will I actually get?

That depends on your market size and the businesses we can reach. Our system can discover and target 50–100+ qualified businesses per month in most regions. The AI assistant works through the list over time, so lead volume is steady and predictable. Free tier customers can test with smaller markets; paid tiers can go bigger.

What's the typical close rate?

Early data shows 5–10% of contacted businesses convert to paying customers at the $49/month price point. Close rates vary by industry, market, and how well the AI's script matches your offer. Your dashboard shows exact conversion rates so you can optimize.

How does the AI phone assistant work?

It answers your business line, understands speech or touch-tone input, can explain pricing and features, and if a prospect is interested, it texts them a secure payment link so they can sign up right then. No transferring to a sales rep—the bot handles the entire close live on the call. You always get a transcript and recording.

Can I try this free first?

Yes. Sign up free to get a customer portal account and test our lead discovery. No payment required. You can add paid billing anytime to unlock outbound calling and SMS follow-ups.

What if a prospect opts out or complains?

Our system automatically respects TCPA opt-outs and CAN-SPAM unsubscribes. If someone texts STOP, we remove them from all future contact. You also get full logs and recordings, so you can refine your pitch or adjust your target market based on feedback.

How is payment confirmed?

Checkout redirects the buyer back to the site, but fulfillment is confirmed only after the Stripe webhook is verified server-side. You're never charged until the payment is complete.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Payments are processed securely through Stripe and we never store your card details. Lead data is encrypted at rest. Your contact information is only used to set up your account and follow up on your requests—never sold or shared.
