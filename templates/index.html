Frequently asked questions

How is payment confirmed?

Checkout redirects the buyer back to the site, but fulfillment is confirmed only after the Stripe webhook is verified server-side.

Can the bot receive and make calls?

Yes. Twilio Voice routes can answer inbound calls with TwiML and you can trigger outbound calls from the backend using your configured Twilio number.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Payments are processed securely through Stripe and we never store your card details. Your contact information is only used to follow up on your request.
